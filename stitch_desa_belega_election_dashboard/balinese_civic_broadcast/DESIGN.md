## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, tactile civic broadcast aesthetic tailored for local democratic events, specifically designed for real-time election monitoring and official tabulation displays. Rejecting modern generic SaaS tropes—such as neon gradients, heavy drop shadows, and pill-shaped floating containers—it synthesizes official administrative governance with the architectural dignity of Balinese village institutions (*Bale Banjar*).

The emotional tone balances solemn civic responsibility with public accessibility. Every surface feels derived from physical paper records, warm stone lintels, and official ink stamps. The overall style is an editorial-civic hybrid: structured, architectural grids, hairline dividers, and disciplined typography ensure total clarity for fast-moving tabular results, live vote tallies, and TPS (Tempat Pemungutan Suara) breakdown feeds across public display screens, mobile devices, and television broadcast overlays.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the physical materials of Gianyar: volcanic river stones, weathered red clay bricks (*bata merah*), ceremonial cloths, and handmade rice paper (*kertas daluwang*). 

- **Primary (`#2F4030` - Deep Moss Green):** The principal mark of civic authority. Applied to the primary interface headers, primary administrative buttons, dominant vote indicators, and active tally cards.
- **Secondary (`#9C4A32` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents Candidate 1. A grounded, earthy red that evokes traditional brickwork without mimicking aggressive error or warning states.
- **Tertiary (`#B8933F` - Balinese Ceremonial Ochre):** Represents Candidate 2. A warm, golden ochre reflecting ceremonial brass and woven gold leafing, maintaining distinct contrast against the terracotta accent.
- **Neutral Surface & Ink Base:**
  - **Background Canvas (`#F3ECDC`):** Warm unbleached rice paper, preventing glare under outdoor public viewing while creating a traditional print foundation.
  - **Surface Default (`#FAF7F0`):** Ivory record paper used for cards, data rows, and input surfaces.
  - **Surface Highlight (`#2F4030`):** Inverted deep moss card for leading candidate tallies, accompanied by off-white ivory text (`#F8F6F0`).
  - **Primary Ink (`#211D17`):** Warm near-black carbon ink for maximum contrast on light grounds.
  - **Secondary Ink (`#595245`):** Muted silt ink for descriptive metadata, timestamps, and secondary column headers.
  - **Structural Hairline (`#D5CBBA`):** Neutral bone-ash divider tone for crisp tabular cell borders and structural margins.

## Typography

The typography unites traditional administrative publishing with high-density data legibility:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the weight, permanence, and ceremonial gravitas of a regional election proclamation. Applied strictly to page headers, village jurisdiction banners, and high-level card titles.
- **Body & Administrative Copy (IBM Plex Sans):** Engineered for objective communication. Used for instructions, status verifications, candidate descriptions, and broadcast logs.
- **Numbers & Live Tally Data (IBM Plex Sans with OpenType Tabular Figures / Fixed-Width Metrics):** All numeric displays (`data-xl`, `data-lg`, `data-md`) must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` or monospaced metrics. This prevents layout jitter during real-time score updates, ensuring percent bars, vote counts, and margin differences remain visually locked into their tabular columns.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in uppercase IBM Plex Sans with slight letter-spacing (`+0.05em`) to evoke formal administrative stamps and official seal typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column rigid desktop grid that collapses into a 4-column stack on mobile viewports. Designed like a traditional broadsheet newspaper crossed with an administrative ledger, space is disciplined, rhythmic, and compact.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width 1440px with 2rem edge margins. Candidate overview panels occupy a 50/50 split or an 8/4 split when paired with real-time TPS reporting feeds. Gutters remain fixed at 1.5rem (`gutter-desktop`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 1.5rem margins and 1rem gutters. Candidate overview cards stack into 2 rows if necessary, retaining side-by-side metric comparisons.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem margins. Complex comparative tables convert to vertically stacked tally modules. Key broadcast numbers maintain prominent scale (`data-lg` or `data-xl`) across the top half of the screen.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** All margins, paddings, and card heights snap to multiples of 4px and 8px. Compact padding inside tabular cells (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`) allows dense data presentation without horizontal overflow.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally avoids diffuse dropshadows, multi-stop radial glows, and floating blur layers. Depth is tactile, structural, and architectural:

- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Spatial hierarchy is established through 1px solid borders tinted in `#D5CBBA` on light surfaces and `#3D523E` on dark surfaces.
- **Tonal Inset Surfaces:** Layering uses paper tones. The background sits at `#F3ECDC`, content containers lift forward via crisp Ivory Paper (`#FAF7F0`), and highlighted data sections use Inverted Moss (`#2F4030`).
- **Structural Stamps:** Critical status badges and real-time status chips employ inset borders (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px ...`) to mimic ink physically stamped onto heavy card stock.
- **Flat Elevation Philosophy:** Z-index overlays are reserved strictly for critical alerts (such as TPS dispute warnings or connection drops). When an overlay is present, it uses a solid 1px hairline border and a flat 2px offset hard shadow (`box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px rgba(33, 29, 23, 0.15)`), preserving the authentic physical ledger feel.

## Shapes

The shape system employs minimal, architectural corner radiuses reflecting hand-trimmed card stock and carved timber. 

- **Level 1 (Soft):** Primary UI elements, data tables, and card containers use `0.25rem` (4px) corner rounding. Large summary panels scale up to a maximum of `0.5rem` (8px). 
- **Prohibited:** Full pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) are forbidden, except for tiny circular live-indicator pulse dots. Rectangular containment communicates official weight and prevents casual consumer-app styling.

## Components

### Buttons & Broadcast Triggers
- **Primary Civic Button:** Solid Deep Moss (`#2F4030`) background, Ivory (`#FAF7F0`) text, 4px border radius, 1px border in `#211D17`. On hover, slightly shifts to `#3B4E3C`.
- **Secondary Action:** Ivory (`#FAF7F0`) background, 1px border in `#D5CBBA`, Primary Ink (`#211D17`) text. On hover, background shifts to `#F3ECDC`.
- **Status Indicator Button:** Includes a left-aligned 6px round status marker indicating active WebSocket synchronization.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- **Standard Candidate Card:** Ivory (`#FAF7F0`) background, 1px solid `#D5CBBA` border, 4px radius. Top accent border (3px solid) colored in the candidate's designated hue (`#9C4A32` or `#B8933F`).
- **Leading Candidate / Highlight Card:** Deep Moss (`#2F4030`) background, `#FAF7F0` text, 1px border in `#211D17`. Data labels render in muted ivory (`#D5CBBA`).
- **TPS Data Row:** Striped or alternating ledger lines with 1px border-bottom (`#D5CBBA`), using tabular numeric figures for vote counts and percentages.

### Live Count Progress Bar
- **Progress Track:** Recessed Ivory (`#EBE4D5`) container, 8px height, 2px radius, 1px hairline border (`#D5CBBA`).
- **Progress Fill:** Split horizontal segments directly comparing Candidate 1 (`#9C4A32`) and Candidate 2 (`#B8933F`), separated by a 1px vertical parchment seam.

### Badges & Official Seals
- **Live Status Chip:** Inset hairline badge with uppercase `label-sm` typography. Displays "DATA MASUK: XX%" or "SUARA SAH" with a live green indicator dot.
- **Candidate Identification Badge:** Numbered badge ("01" / "02") utilizing Playfair Display Bold inside a 28x28px square with 4px border radius and 1px hairline frame.

### Form Inputs & Filters (TPS Lookup)
- **Input Field:** Ivory (`#FAF7F0`) background, 1px solid `#D5CBBA` border, 4px radius, 12px horizontal padding. Focused state switches border to Deep Moss (`#2F4030`) with no outer glow rings.
- **Select / Dropdown:** Matching input styling with a custom carved-down chevron indicator in Secondary Ink (`#595245`).
